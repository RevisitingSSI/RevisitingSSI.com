The Global Digital Collaboration Conference 2026 (GDC 26) was a
conference located in Geneva, Switzerland that focused on
international collaboration with a focus on digital identity and
credentials.

Martina Kolpondinos gave a presentation on **September 1, 2026** on
"Self-Sovereign Identity: The Next Ten Years". The Chatham House rules
prevented a recording of the presentation, but the slides are
available.
